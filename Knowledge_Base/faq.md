# Saathi Sneha Care – Customer Support FAQ

## 1. What services does Saathi Sneha Care provide?

Saathi Sneha Care provides home-care services such as caregiver support, medication management, doctor consultations, physiotherapy, hospital escort, post-hospital care, chronic disease monitoring, and wellness checks.

## 2. How can I get a caregiver for my parent?

You can contact Saathi Sneha Care and provide information about your parent's care needs. The care team can help you choose a suitable care plan and arrange the required support.

## 3. How much does the service cost?

The cost depends on the selected care plan and the care services required. Please contact Saathi Sneha Care or check the available care plans for current pricing.

## 4. How can I pay for the service?

Customers outside Nepal can pay using international credit or debit cards through Stripe. Customers in Nepal can use Khalti or cash payments.

## 5. How can I cancel my care plan?

You can cancel your care plan at any time. Cancellation does not have a penalty, and the change takes effect from the next billing cycle.

## 6. What should I do if I have a problem with my caregiver or service?

Please contact the Saathi Sneha Care support team and explain the problem. Provide relevant details about the service or caregiver so the support team can investigate and assist you.
